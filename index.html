Bengaluru Waste Segregation Framework & Bye-Law Citations

BBMP SWM Bye-Laws 2020
1. BBMP SWM Bye-Laws 2020

Mandatory 3-way segregation across all homes and businesses: Category A (Wet / Food Waste), Category B (Dry / Recyclables), and Category C (Domestic Sanitary / Hazardous).

Ref: BBMP Bye-Laws Schedule-I
2. MoEFCC SWM Rules 2016

Directs that non-recyclable multi-layered plastics with high energy value be baled at Dry Waste Collection Centres (DWCCs) for co-processing in cement kilns (Refuse-Derived Fuel).

Ref: MoEFCC SWM Rules (Rule 4)
3. Plastic Waste Rules 2016/2021

Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) standards for Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) on multi-layer flexible sachets and plastic recovery quotas.

Ref: CPCB EPR Guidelines 2022
